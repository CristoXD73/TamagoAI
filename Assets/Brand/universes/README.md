## Provenance

- **Provided by the owner in chat, 2026-09-29.** Creator, tool and licence are *to be recorded by the owner*.
  That's required before any public use, as for every character asset (`docs/UPSTREAM_REUSE.md`; AGENTS.md §2,
  no third-party artwork without a clear licence).
- PNG, RGB, no alpha. Cut them out before placing them on anything but a matching background.
- **Before public use of `tamago-sprout.png`:** the stem-and-leaf-on-the-head look is close to Nintendo's
  Pikmin. That's fine inside the app for ourselves; worth a second look before marketing.
