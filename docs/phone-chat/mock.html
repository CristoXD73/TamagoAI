How do I make sourdough at home?
▤ Full answer
You need a starter, flour, water and salt, and about a day. 1. Feed your starter the night before. 2. Mix 500 g flour, 350 g water and 100 g starter. Rest 1 hour. 3. Add 10 g salt, then fold every 30 minutes for 2 hours. 4. Let it rise, shape it, and chill it overnight. 5. Bake at 250 °C in a covered pot, 20 min + 20 min uncovered.
📱 Saved here for you
Why does it need to chill overnight?
Cold slows the yeast, so the flavor gets deeper and the dough is easier to score.
